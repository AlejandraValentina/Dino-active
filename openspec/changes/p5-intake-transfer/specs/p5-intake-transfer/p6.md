## P6 requirements

### Requirement: four conservative pseudo-species
P6 SHALL transport exactly `fresh_air`, `fuel`, `residual` and `burned` as
conservative masses. Their sum SHALL equal total mass in every admissible 0D
and 1D state. No reaction, heat release, combustion chemistry or composition
dependent EOS is introduced.

### Requirement: shared stage flux and donor semantics
Species transport SHALL use the mass flux resolved by the same SSPRK2 stage
interface solve as the gasdynamic state. Forward and reverse flow SHALL use the
actual upstream donor composition; no sign or donor postprocessing is allowed.

### Requirement: ledgers and topology
Independent global ledgers SHALL be exposed for all four species over the full
P5-C topology. Atmospheric composition defaults to fresh_air=1 and all other
species zero. Transfer, short-circuit exhaust and reverse-flow accounting SHALL
be covered by bounded fixtures.

### Requirement: admissibility and restart
Species masses SHALL remain finite and nonnegative, sum to total mass, and be
preserved by checkpoint/restart and deterministic replay. P4 remains
`BLOCKED`/`NOT_GRANTED`; all P6 evidence is `CONDITIONAL_ON_P4`.
