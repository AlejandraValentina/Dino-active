# Unified Dino scientific reliability v1

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: preserve native scientific semantics
The platform SHALL retain independent 2T and 4T physical models, configurations,
result identities, cycle conventions and maturity records while sharing
application infrastructure.

#### Scenario: a common power indicator is displayed
- **WHEN** a result belongs to a native 4T engine
- **THEN** its 720-degree cycle convention SHALL remain distinct from the 2T convention
- **AND** a common label SHALL NOT transfer scientific validation between families.

### Requirement: audit-led prospective scheduling
The plan SHALL reference the frozen audit before its creation and SHALL map all
findings to scoped future acceptance. Historical criteria and evidence SHALL
remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: the old A/B software gate passed
- **WHEN** later audit probes confirm input or provenance defects
- **THEN** the prior narrow PASS SHALL remain historical
- **AND** the new findings SHALL block an unqualified reliability claim until resolved with new evidence.

### Requirement: separate maturity and evidence gates
The platform SHALL keep software, numerical, physical-identification,
calibration, experimental and predictive gates separate for each engine family.

#### Scenario: a synthetic 4T pair is periodic
- **WHEN** its registered monitor and provenance checks pass
- **THEN** it MAY establish computed model sensitivity within its scope
- **AND** it SHALL NOT close experimental or predictive validation.

### Requirement: explicit activation and budget control
Publishing this plan SHALL NOT activate implementation, a scientific campaign,
a build or an old continuation. Each future stage SHALL carry its own scope,
criteria, resource estimate and authorized budget before execution.

#### Scenario: 2T E1.3 remains over its approved budget
- **WHEN** the unified plan is adopted for scheduling
- **THEN** E1.3 SHALL remain unactivated and blocked
- **AND** no 4T or audit budget SHALL be transferred to it.

### Requirement: independent per-engine measured validation
Each family SHALL require a complete matched measured dataset, separate
calibration and holdout, preregistered criteria and uncertainty before a
predictive claim. Synthetic assumptions SHALL NOT replace missing measurements.

#### Scenario: measured crankcase or valve data are missing
- **WHEN** the required validation package is incomplete
- **THEN** validation SHALL remain blocked by data
- **AND** independent software or numerical preparation MAY proceed only within its own activated scope.
