Dear McClay Library Customer Services,

Could you please forward this enquiry to the relevant thesis, engineering or archival staff at Queen's University Belfast? I am seeking surviving records associated with W. L. Cahoon's November 1971 PhD thesis, *Unsteady gas flow through a naturally aspirated two stroke internal combustion engine*, specifically for the Greeves 380 MK.1. I already have the institutional thesis PDF and am not requesting another copy:

https://pure.qub.ac.uk/en/studentTheses/unsteady-gas-flow-through-a-naturally-aspirated-two-stroke-intern/

I am trying to establish whether any original input sheets, corrections, laboratory records or measurement files survive for the following three points:

1. **MK.1 crankcase free volume.** The specification table (printed p. 11; repository PDF p. 19) and the THROUGH FLOW input sheet (PDF p. 253) state a crankcase compression ratio of 1.6. Is the ratio defined as maximum divided by minimum crankcase volume? Which cavities and passages were included? A documented absolute free volume at a stated crank angle, or the measured volume law and its boundaries, would resolve the ambiguity; full CAD is not needed.

2. **Which exhaust geometry was fitted to the measured MK.1?** The specification table (PDF p. 19) lists D1/D9/D2/D3 = 1.94/2.92/3.75/3.75 in and L9 = 22 in. The MK.1 THROUGH FLOW example identified at 4400 RPM (Appendix 2, PDF pp. 253–255) lists 1.95/2.94/3.625/3.625 in and K9 = 23 in. Is there a revision note, erratum or as-tested sheet identifying which dimensions apply to the experimental runs around 6000 RPM? I do not want to combine these alternatives or infer an as-tested geometry.

3. **Records for the 6000 RPM point.** Table 4.1 (PDF p. 79) reports MK.1 brake results at 6000 RPM, and Fig. 4.13 (PDF p. 91) shows a cylinder-pressure trace labelled 6000 RPM. If retained, could you advise whether the raw pressure samples/trace values and corresponding brake, airflow and fuel records exist for that same acquisition? The actual speed and load, ignition setting, fuel, ambient conditions, SAE correction applied, instrument calibration, sensor positions and any repeatability or uncertainty notes would help identify a coherent measured point. If the trace and table are from different runs, their individual run conditions would be useful.

I would be grateful for any surviving record, a catalogue reference, or confirmation that these materials are no longer held. Please also advise access, copying and reuse conditions; no reproduction or reuse permission is presumed. A negative finding or referral to another institutional contact would be useful.
