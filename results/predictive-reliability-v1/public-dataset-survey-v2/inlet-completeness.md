# Inlet candidate: quantitative adoption not justified yet

SELF_REVIEW, read-only. Source and transcribed values are preserved in the
previous receipt/candidate, not rebound or overwritten. Relevant Engine A tables,
geometry, probe and inlet-method pages were visually inspected (PDF pp. 1–6).
The remaining Engine B/combustion results are not mixed into this case.

**Decision: retain a partial subsystem candidate, not an executable validation
fixture.** Its oscillation period is an inlet-wave observable; it is not a
demonstration of engine cycle-to-cycle periodicity, a brake-power curve or an
independent validation of combustion/scavenging.

Engineering requirements still not established by this bounded inspection:

- Matched initial/boundary thermal and pressure conditions, including the
  thermodynamic compliance used for an absolute resonance prediction.
- Complete imposed piston/crankcase-volume history and its rod geometry, or an
  independently documented equivalent actuator history.
- Inclusion convention for the reported crankcase volume, so separately modelled
  connected port/duct spaces are not double counted.
- Adequate port area law, end correction/loss definitions and the relevant
  surrounding gas paths, or measured boundary histories that make an isolated
  inlet test well posed.
- Measurement uncertainty, phase convention and any figure-readout uncertainty
  for waveform comparisons. Printed decimal precision is not a U95 interval.

These are inputs/identifiability requirements, not evidence that the original
experiment is defective. The material is better suited to testing an inlet
mechanism than to validating a full Dino power curve. Supplying guessed inputs,
adjusting them to the four periods, then testing those same periods would be
calibration rather than independent prediction. No such fit was made.

No new reference replaces X30, and no X30/synthetic geometry is transferred into
this candidate. A future quantitative comparison requires the finite missing
input/uncertainty package above; qualitative trends can be discussed with their
limited scope, without declaring a validation PASS. No solver execution or new
data digitization was performed for this assessment.
